Delete Your SakuraLog Account

SakuraLog, an anime tracker for iPhone and Android. Last updated: November 3, 2026

You can delete your SakuraLog account and all the data stored with it at any time. This page explains how.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open SakuraLog and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  3. Tap Account Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. It cannot be undone, and there is no recovery period.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address, username and sign-in credentials.
  • Your whole watchlist: statuses, episode progress, ratings, notes and tags.
  • Your profile and your profile picture, if you uploaded one.
  • Your in-app notifications and notification preferences, and the push notification token for your devices.
  • Any feedback you sent through the app.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept. Short-lived rate-limit records, which are used to block abusive traffic and are not linked to your account, are removed automatically within one day. Our authentication provider may keep standard security logs for a limited period.

If you can't open the app

If you can no longer use the app, for example because you uninstalled it or lost your device, email us and we will delete the account for you. Write from the email address you signed up with and use the subject line "Delete my account":

support.sakuralog@gmail.com

We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.
